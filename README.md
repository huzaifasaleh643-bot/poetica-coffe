# Poetica Canvas

Create a premium, high-end professional multi-location website for "Poetica Coffee" based in New York (Brooklyn & Manhattan). The design must be deeply artistic, sophisticated, and minimalist, perfectly reflecting their brand philosophy of "Radical Hospitality" and "Coffee as Art".

---

### 🎨 BRAND IDENTITY, THEME & NATURE
- **Nature/Vibe:** Artistic, independent specialty coffeehouse, intellectual, welcoming, and deeply rooted in community & refugee solidarity. NOT a fast-food or corporate chain aesthetic.
- **Color Palette (Manhattan/Brooklyn Artistic Look):**
  - Primary Background: Creamy Warm Off-White (#F9F6F0) for an inviting, editorial feel.
  - Charcoal / Dark Onyx (#1A1A1A or #222222) for premium text, headers, and structural elements.
  - Muted Amber / Honey Gold (#D4AF37 or #C69214) used subtly as accent colors for specific signature highlights.
- **Typography & Font Style:**
  - Headings: Elegant, classic, high-end Serif font (e.g., Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond style) to reflect the "Poetry/Literary" theme.
  - Body Text: Clean, highly readable, minimalist Sans-serif font (e.g., Inter or DM Sans style).

---

### 🏛️ WEBSITE STRUCTURE & SECTIONS

1. **Navigation Bar (Sticky & Transparent):**
   - Left: Elegant typographic logo "Poetica Coffee".
   - Center/Right Links: Home, Locations, Menu, Our Story, Contact.
   - CTA Button: "Order Direct & Save" (Emphasize ordering directly instead of third-party delivery).

2. **Hero Section (High-Impact Editorial Design):**
   - Main Heading: "Radical Hospitality. Handcrafted Coffee." or "The Poetry of Specialty Coffee in New York."
   - Sub-heading: "Experience artisanal espresso, organic matcha, and a sanctuary of art and solidarity across Brooklyn & Manhattan."
   - CTA Buttons: Two buttons side-by-side: [Explore Menu] (Scrolls to menu) and [Order Direct & Save] (Primary colored button).

3. **Multi-Location Hub (Interactive Grid or Dropdown):**
   - Showcase their 7 dynamic neighborhoods and 1 mobile truck with clean cards:
     - Carroll Gardens (Smith Street - The 2020 Original)
     - East Village (2nd Avenue - Manhattan's historic Gem Spa corner)
     - Williamsburg (Lorimer Street)
     - Windsor Terrace (Prospect Ave)
     - Prospect Park West
     - 7th Avenue
     - Caton Ave
     - The Poetica Truck (Prospect Park Entrance)
   - Each card should show a dummy "Open Status", Hours ("Daily: 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM"), and a button "Select This Branch".

4. **The Signature Literary Menu (Interactive Tabbed Section):**
   - **Signature Drinks Tab:** - "The Poética" (Handcrafted Signature Blend) - $5.25
     - Honey Cardamom Latte - $5.50
     - Iced Poetica - $7.08
     - Blooming Matcha - $6.00
     - London Fog - $4.20
   - **Standard Coffee Tab:**
     - Espresso ($3.25), Cortado ($4.46), Cappuccino ($5.14), Flat White ($4.98), Cafe Latte ($5.51), Drip Coffee ($3.41).
   - **Bakery & Bites Tab:**
     - Chocolate Croissants ($4.46), Breakfast Sandwiches, Artisanal Cookies, Premium Yogurt Bowls ($6.24).
   - **Retail Beans Shop (Mini Grid):**
     - Showcase dynamic product cards for "Poetica House Blend Bags ($18.36)" and "Bulk 5-lbs Coffee Bags ($96.51)" with an "Add to Cart" function.

5. **Our Core Values (The Brand Story Impact Section):**
   - Beautiful editorial split layout or clean text block highlighting:
     - **Banned Books Shelf:** Every branch hosts a dedicated collection of free-to-read literature that has been challenged or banned.
     - **Refugee Solidarity:** Highlighting Poetica's mission to support and employ immigrants and refugees building lives in NYC.

6. **Footer Section:**
   - Clean columns containing: Location links, Operating hours, Social tags (@poeticacoffee), and a powerful micro-copy: "Skip the aggregators. Order directly from us to support independent coffee and radical hospitality."

### 🛠️ TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Use React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide Icons.
- Implement highly polished, smooth interactive states (hover scales, tab transitions, dropdown changes).
- Maintain absolute mobile responsiveness with flawless navigation padding.
- Ensure zero third-party broken links; keep all interactions in-house on this beautiful prototype.
